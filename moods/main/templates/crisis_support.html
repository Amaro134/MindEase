Get Immediate Help

If you or someone you know is in danger or experiencing a mental health crisis, reach out for help immediately.

🇺🇬 Uganda Crisis Support

  • Uganda Suicide Prevention Helpline: +256 788 662 663
  • Mental Health Uganda: +256 414 532 483 / +256 772 539 551
  • Butabika Hospital Emergency: +256 414 504 376
  • Uganda Police Emergency: 999 or 112

🌍 International Hotlines

  • Suicide Crisis Helpline (Global): findahelpline.com
  • US - 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988
  • UK - Samaritans: 116 123
  • Canada - Talk Suicide Canada: 1-833-456-4566
  • India - AASRA Helpline: 91-9820466726
