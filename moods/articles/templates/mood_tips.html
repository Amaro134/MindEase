Mood Tracking Tips

Track Daily

Make it a habit to log your mood every day, even if it’s just a word or a short sentence. Consistency helps identify patterns over time.

Use a Mood Scale

Rate your mood on a scale of 1-10 or use emojis to capture your feelings. This helps quantify changes and track progress.

Identify Triggers

Note situations, people, or activities that impact your mood positively or negatively. Awareness of triggers allows better emotional management.

Reflect and Review

Take time weekly to review your mood logs. Look for patterns, improvements, or recurring challenges.

Combine with Journaling

Writing about your experiences, thoughts, and feelings can provide deeper insight into your emotional state.

Set Reminders

Use phone alarms or app notifications to remind yourself to log your mood. Small nudges help you stay consistent.
